# 🎥 Quick Guide: Upload Videos to Google Drive (5 Minutes)

## Step 1: Upload Your 4 Videos

1. **Go to Google Drive:** https://drive.google.com
2. **Click "New" → "File upload"**
3. **Select and upload these 4 files:**
   - Part_1_upd.mp4
   - UAV_Part_2_AIrborne_Networking.mp4
   - Part_3.mp4
   - Part_4.mp4

## Step 2: Make Each Video Shareable

**For EACH video, do this:**

1. **Right-click the video** → Click "Share"
2. **Click "Change to anyone with the link"**
3. **Make sure it says "Viewer"** (not Editor)
4. **Click "Copy link"**
5. **Paste the link somewhere** (Notepad, Word, etc.)

## Step 3: Get the Embed URLs

**For each link you copied, you'll see something like:**
```
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ABC123XYZ456/view?usp=sharing
```

**Extract the FILE_ID** (the part between `/d/` and `/view`):
```
1ABC123XYZ456
```

**Convert to embed URL:**
```
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ABC123XYZ456/preview
```
